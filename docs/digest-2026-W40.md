## Zone 3 · The rest of the field { #low-relevance }

_What else moved this week, by theme:_

- **Agentic Safety & Runtime Guardrails** — Focuses on runtime monitoring, access control, and outcome validation to prevent tool-using agents from taking unauthorized or destructive execution steps. Key examples include PROACT-Agent for real-time circuit-breaking and AGATE for provenance-based execution boundaries.
- **Adversarial Robustness & Jailbreak Defense** — Explores vulnerabilities to indirect prompt injection, multimodal jailbreaks, and activation-steering bypasses across multi-turn workflows. Directions like CounterSteer and the pikit toolkit develop adaptive defenses to suppress untrusted instructions.
- **Alignment, Preference Optimization & Reward Hacking** — Investigates post-training methods like RLVR, DPO, and on-policy self-distillation while tackling reward hacking and specification gaming. Frameworks like CATCH and STAR-GRPO establish testbeds and robust advantage estimators to maintain alignment stability.
- **Mechanistic Interpretability & Circuit Analysis** — Applies sparse autoencoders, activation patching, and representation geometry to locate refusal mechanisms, reasoning circuits, and concept manifolds. Papers such as D-Scope and PULSE map internal representations to explain model decisions and control steering side effects.
- **Multi-Agent Security & Coordination Dynamics** — Examines failure modes in collaborative systems, including cascading misinformation, authority escalation, and agent-to-agent prompt injection. Studies such as MADBench and AuthorityLens evaluate debate security and permission structures in multi-agent networks.
- **Safety Evaluation, Benchmarking & Sycophancy** — Establishes process-level benchmarks for long-horizon agent capabilities, cybersecurity tool use, domain compliance, and behavioral flaws like sycophancy under false accusations. Examples include KaliBench for CLI tool proficiency and CAVE-Bench for gaslight vulnerability.
- **Governance, Watermarking & Privacy Preservation** — Addresses regulatory compliance, verifiable private execution, machine unlearning, and model provenance. Research like Bits Under ZK-LLM and Witness Overlap explores zero-knowledge inference and weight-space lineage to support auditability and IP safety.
