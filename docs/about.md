# About

This digest is tailored to Aaron's work on international AI coordination and
verification, with technical research on catastrophic AI risk as its backbone.

Scheduled dot runs start on **Sundays at 10:17 UTC**, covering the preceding
seven days. Publication follows source-coverage and editorial checks, so an
edition may appear later when a source needs to be retried.

## How the digest is prepared

1. Collect new work from arXiv, the configured researcher watchlist, lab and
   policy publications, selected newsletters, and research forums.
2. Apply the configured keyword, author, source, and date filters. Freeze the
   candidate set together with the current review rubric and reader feedback.
3. Dot reviews every admitted candidate against Aaron's priorities. Shortlisted
   items receive a deeper read when source text is available. Limited previews,
   truncated excerpts, and unavailable full texts remain identified as such.
4. Check that every candidate has a decision and that the edition is grounded
   in the captured evidence. Build and verify the site before publishing it.
   If collection or review is unfinished, preserve the draft and resume it.

The project is open source: [browse the code](https://github.com/AI-Safety-Weekly/ai-safety-digest).

## Relevance to Aaron

| Tier | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **High — direct lane** | International AI coordination, frontier-AI or compute governance, and mechanisms for verifying that countries or labs honor AI commitments. Verification of AI agreements is the central priority. |
| **Medium — technical backbone** | Catastrophic-risk research that makes coordination necessary: dangerous capabilities, scheming, deception, loss of control, AI-control methods, and relevant frontier-lab safety reports. |
| **Low — outside the lane** | Other substantive AI/ML or safety work, summarized in the rest-of-the-field section rather than treated as a direct recommendation. Exceptionally significant outside-lane breakthroughs may be highlighted separately. |
| **Off-topic** | Material outside the rubric, including non-AI work sharing governance or verification vocabulary, general applications without a safety angle, and exclusions established by reader feedback. Omitted from the edition. |

These are relevance judgments for one reader, not a ranking of a paper's overall
quality. A title alone does not establish a high or medium rating. Generic
cryptography or software-security work is not direct-lane verification merely
because it uses words such as “attestation” or “trust.”

A separate capabilities watch tracks qualifying frontier releases. Rare
outside-lane breakthroughs are distinguished from routine novelty.

## Sources and feedback

Author and keyword settings live in `config/authors.yml` and
`config/keywords.yml`; publication sources are in `config/lab_sources.yml`.
Changes and reader feedback inform subsequent runs. Author feeds depend on
resolved author records; unresolved identities remain disclosed coverage gaps
rather than being guessed.
